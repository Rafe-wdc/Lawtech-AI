exercise, with reasonable competence in the given case, the skill which he did possess. The standard is not of the
highest expert skill, but that of a reasonably competent practitioner in that field.

17. That the Defendant No. 1 committed a gross breach of this duty of care. The negligence is manifest in:
a. Failing to exercise reasonable surgical skill and care during the operation, leading to the bile duct injury.
b. Failing to identify the injury intra-operatively, which is a critical aspect of surgical competence.
