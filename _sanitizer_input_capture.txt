### Section 299 vs Section 300 — Indian Penal Code, 1860 and Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023

| **Aspect** | **Section 299 IPC (Culpable Homicide)** | **Section 300 IPC (Murder)** | **Section 100 BNS (Culpable Homicide)** | **Section 101 BNS (Murder)** |
---
| **Primary Purpose** | Defines the lesser offence of causing death with specific intent or knowledge. | Defines the graver offence of murder, a species of culpable homicide with higher culpability. | Defines the lesser offence of causing death with specific intent or knowledge. | Defines the graver offence of murder, a species of culpable homicide with higher culpability. |
| **Key Conditions** | Causing death with:<br>1. Intention to cause death.<br>2. Intention to cause bodily injury likely to cause death.<br>3. Knowledge that the act is likely to cause death. | Culpable homicide is murder if:<br>1. Intention to cause death.<br>2. Intention to cause bodily injury known to be likely to cause death.<br>3. Intention to cause bodily injury sufficient in ordinary course of nature to cause death.<br>4. Knowledge that the act is imminently dangerous and must, in all probability, cause death or likely death. | Causing death with:<br>1. Intention to cause death.<br>2. Intention to cause bodily injury likely to cause death.<br>3. Knowledge that the act is likely to cause death. | Culpable homicide is murder if:<br>1. Intention to cause death.<br>2. Intention to cause bodily injury known to be likely to cause death.<br>3. Intention to cause bodily injury sufficient in ordinary course of nature to cause death.<br>4. Knowledge that the act is imminently dangerous and must, in all probability, cause death or likely death. |
| **Relationship** | Broader category; all murders are culpable homicides, but not all culpable homicides are murders. | A more aggravated form of culpable homicide, with specific aggravating circumstances. | Broader category; all murders are culpable homicides, but not all culpable homicides are murders. | A more aggravated form of culpable homicide, with specific aggravating circumstances. |
