A SUIT FOR PARTITION, DECLARATION AND PERMANENT INJUNCTION

## Plaintiff vs. Defendants

The Plaintiff abovenamed submits this plaint, praying to state as follows:

1. That the Plaintiff, Ms. Rupa, is the legally adopted daughter of Late [Father's Name] and the Defendant, Mrs. [Mother's Name]. The Plaintiff was adopted as per all legal rites and ceremonies and has, since her adoption, been accorded the status of a natural-born daughter, enjoying all rights and privileges thereof.

2. That the Plaintiff's adoptive father, Late [Father's Name], passed away intestate approximately five years ago on or about [Date of Father's Death], leaving behind the Plaintiff and the Defendant as his only legal heirs.

3. That the deceased, Late [Father's Name], had no other children, natural-born or adopted, besides the Plaintiff. Therefore, the Plaintiff and the Defendant are the sole Class I legal heirs of the deceased as defined under Section 8 of the Hindu Succession Act, 1956, and are entitled to succeed to his estate in equal shares.

## A SUIT FOR PARTITION AND TEMPORARY INJUNCTION

4. That during his lifetime, the said Late [Father's Name] had, out of his own earnings, acquired two residential flats situated in Pune, which are his self-acquired properties. The details of the said properties (hereinafter "the Suit Properties") are more particularly described in the Schedule of Properties annexed hereto.
- **Property A:** Flat No. [Flat Number], on [Floor Number], in the building known as [Building Name], located at [Address of Property A], Pune.
- **Property B:** Flat No. [Flat Number], on [Floor Number], in the building known as [Building Name], located at [Address of Property B], Pune.

5. That upon the intestate demise of Late [Father's Name], the Plaintiff and the Defendant, being the only two Class I heirs, each inherited an equal, undivided one-half (1/2) share, right, title, and interest in the aforementioned Suit Properties. The Plaintiff is in joint possession of the Suit Properties along with the Defendant.

6. The Plaintiff submits that although her name has not yet been mutated in the property records or the records of the concerned co-operative housing societies, she has acquired a vested right and interest in the Suit Properties by operation of law upon the death of her father.

7. That recently, the Defendant's conduct towards the Plaintiff has turned hostile and adverse to the Plaintiff's interests. The Defendant is actively attempting to dispossess the Plaintiff and has expressed a clear intention to illegally sell, transfer, alienate, or create third-party rights in the Suit Properties in favour of her cousin, with the mala fide intention of depriving the Plaintiff of her rightful share.

8. That the Plaintiff submits that if the Defendant is not restrained by an order of temporary injunction from
alienating or creating any third-party interest in the Suit Properties during the pendency of this suit, it will cause
irreparable loss and injury to the Plaintiff and may lead to a multiplicity of proceedings. The Plaintiff has a prima
facie case, the balance of convenience is in her favour, and she is entitled to the reliefs sought under Order XXXIX
Rules 1 and 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908.

## Facts of the Case

9. That on or about [Date of Demand], the Plaintiff approached the Defendant and requested an amicable partition of the Suit Properties and demanded her legitimate one-half (1/2) share in the same. However, the Defendant flatly refused to partition the properties and denied the Plaintiff's rights, claiming sole ownership.

10. That the Plaintiff has a credible apprehension that the Defendant, in collusion with her cousin, is taking active steps to alienate, sell, mortgage, or create third-party rights in the Suit Properties. The Defendant's actions are illegal, unjust, and calculated to defeat the Plaintiff's vested interest in the estate of her deceased father.

11. That the cause of action for the present suit first arose on [Date of Demand], when the Plaintiff demanded partition and the Defendant refused the same. The cause of action is continuing and accrues from day to day as the Defendant persists in her refusal and continues her attempts to illegally transfer the Suit Properties. Hence, the suit filed today is well within the period of limitation as prescribed by the Limitation Act, 1963.

## Legal Provisions and Reliefs Sought

12. The Plaintiff submits that as the adopted daughter of the deceased, she is a Class I legal heir under Section 8 of the Hindu Succession Act, 1956. Upon the intestate death of her father, she is legally entitled to a one-half (1/2) undivided share in his self-acquired properties, i.e., the Suit Properties.

13. That the Plaintiff is entitled to a decree of declaration, declaring her to be the owner of a one-half (1/2) share in the Suit Properties. The Plaintiff is also entitled to a decree for partition of the Suit Properties by metes and bounds and to be put in separate possession of her share.

14. That for the purpose of temporary injunction, the Plaintiff has a strong prima facie case, the balance of convenience lies in her favour, and she will suffer irreparable loss and injury if the Defendant is not restrained from creating third-party rights during the pendency of the suit. The relief of temporary injunction is sought under Order XXXIX Rules 1 and 2 read with Section 151 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908.

15. That the Suit Properties are situated in Pune, within the territorial jurisdiction of this Hon'ble Court. Therefore, this Hon'ble Court has the jurisdiction to entertain, try, and decide the present suit.

16. That for the purposes of court fees and jurisdiction, the suit is valued for the relief of partition at [Valuation Amount for Partition] and for the relief of permanent injunction at Rs. 1,000/-. The requisite court fee as per the Maharashtra Court Fees Act, 1959, has been paid herewith. The Plaintiff undertakes to pay any additional court fee that this Hon'ble Court may direct.

## Prayer

17. The Plaintiff, therefore, prays that this Hon'ble Court be pleased to:
(a) Pass a decree of declaration, declaring that the Plaintiff is the lawful owner of an undivided one-half (1/2) share, right, title, and interest in the Suit Properties, more particularly described in the Schedule of Properties annexed hereto;

(b) Pass a preliminary decree for partition of the Suit Properties, declaring that the Plaintiff is entitled to a one-half (1/2) share therein;

(c) Pass a final decree for partition of the Suit Properties by metes and bounds, and put the Plaintiff in separate, vacant, and peaceful possession of her one-half (1/2) share;

(d) For the purpose of effecting the partition, be pleased to appoint a Court Commissioner or any other fit and proper person to divide the Suit Properties and submit a report to this Hon'ble Court;

(e) Pass a decree for permanent injunction, perpetually restraining the Defendant, her agents, servants, assigns, or any person claiming through or under her, from selling, transferring, alienating, mortgaging, gifting, leasing, or creating any third-party rights, title, or interest of any nature whatsoever in respect of the Suit Properties or any part thereof;

(f) Pending the hearing and final disposal of this suit, be pleased to grant a temporary injunction restraining the Defendant, her agents, servants, assigns, or any person claiming through or under her, from selling, transferring, alienating, mortgaging, or creating any third-party rights, title, or interest in the Suit Properties, as prayed for in the accompanying application under Order XXXIX Rules 1 and 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908;

(g) Award the costs of this suit to the Plaintiff from the Defendant;

(h) Grant any other and further reliefs as this Hon'ble Court may deem fit and proper in the facts and circumstances of the case.
