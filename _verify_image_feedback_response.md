-   **Relevance:** The evidence must be relevant to the facts in issue.
-   **Authenticity:** The evidence must be proven to be what it purports to be. This often involves demonstrating the integrity of the data and the process by which it was collected and preserved.
-   **Reliability:** The electronic record must be shown to have been produced by a computer system that was operating properly, or if not, that the deviation did not affect the accuracy of the record.
-   **Certificate:** Often, a certificate from a person in charge of the computer system or device is required, attesting to the proper functioning of the system and the accuracy of the electronic record.
